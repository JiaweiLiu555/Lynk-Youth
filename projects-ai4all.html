Rectal Cancer AI · Early Research

Colvera AI

From the Medical AI track

Growing from Jiawei and Rowan's work in Stanford HAI's AI4ALL Medical AI track, Colvera AI is a hospital-facing research platform focused on a specific challenge in rectal-cancer care: determining whether tissue remaining after chemotherapy and radiation is harmless scar or hidden residual cancer. It combines MRI, endoscopy, examination findings, treatment history, and confirmed outcomes to help multidisciplinary teams interpret disagreement between tests and evaluate surgery versus organ-preserving watch-and-wait care.

Legal AI · In Development

Louis AI

An applied-AI continuation

Co-founded by Jiawei and Alistair, Louis AI carries the applied, human-centered spirit of AI4ALL into legal work. The locally run assistant is being designed with lawyer feedback to support small firms with supervised drafting, research, summarization, and organization.
