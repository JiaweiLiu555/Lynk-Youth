About Us

Lynk Youth Initiative is a youth-led nonprofit dedicated to service, cultural understanding, and community connection.

Our Mission

We are a student-led organization that amplifies youth voices, ideas, and actions for students from around the world through impactful projects.

Our members come from very different backgrounds, experiences, and communities, but we are united by a shared commitment to leadership, compassion, and making a meaningful impact.

Jiawei Liu

Jiawei is a student at Lexington High School and the founder of the Lynk Youth Initiative. He is passionate about community service, leadership, and connecting with others through meaningful projects. His interests include math, music, sports, reading, and public speaking. Through Lynk Youth Initiative, he hopes to help bring young people together to create positive change in both local and global communities.
