Public Speaking & Debate

2025 Summer Public Speaking Workshop

Summer 2025 · Linyi, China

Jiawei led three hands-on talks for more than 800 participants on public speaking, speaking with confidence, and debate. The program moved from instruction to practice, concluding with a live debate in which participants put their new skills to work.

International Education

2024 Summer Speeches

Summer 2024 · Linyi, China

Across five school visits, Jiawei spoke with more than 300 participants about everyday life and primary education in the United States. The series created a direct exchange through which students could learn about another culture and ask questions firsthand.
