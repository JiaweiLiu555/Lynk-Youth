Clinical AI · In Development

Colvera AI

Current Project

Colvera AI is a clinical decision-support platform being developed by Jiawei, Rowan, and Ishan. It analyzes longitudinal medical records to help care teams identify the safest and most informative next step—such as a test, procedure, or specialist referral—when cancer may be present. The project grew from Jiawei and Rowan's work in Stanford HAI's AI4ALL Medical AI track.

Legal AI · In Development

Louis AI

Current Project

Jiawei and Alistair co-founded Louis AI, a locally run assistant designed to help small law firms with supervised drafting, research, summarization, and organization. The team is interviewing lawyers and using their feedback to guide responsible development.
