Rectal Cancer AI · Early Research

Colvera AI

Current Project

Colvera AI is a hospital-facing research platform being developed by Jiawei and Rowan to help clinicians distinguish harmless post-treatment scar tissue from hidden residual rectal cancer. By combining MRI, endoscopy, examination findings, treatment history, and confirmed patient outcomes, its discrepancy-aware approach aims to clarify conflicting test results and support safer decisions between surgery and organ-preserving watch-and-wait care. The platform is designed for multidisciplinary clinician review, not autonomous diagnosis or treatment.

Private Legal AI · In Development

Louis AI

Current Project

Jiawei and Alistair co-founded Louis AI, a private assistant designed for solo attorneys and small law firms. Installed directly on a firm's own computer, it uses a law-tuned open-weight model so client information and matter details remain local rather than being sent to outside AI providers. Lawyers can use it by text or through a simple desktop interface to draft routine client updates, summarize documents and email threads, extract parties, dates, and deadlines, organize matter notes, and prepare defined tasks overnight. Every output remains a draft for attorney review; Louis AI is a supervised productivity tool, not a replacement for legal judgment or high-stakes analysis.
