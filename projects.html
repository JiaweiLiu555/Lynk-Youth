Cancer App

Current Project

Jiawei and Rowan are developing a tool that tracks oral lesions over time using smartphone images and patient information. This project is based upon work done by the two in the Stanford AI4ALL program.

In Development

Louis AI

Current Project

Jiawei and Alistair have co-founded Louis AI (louislaw.ai), a locally run AI assistant to help small law firms with supervised drafting, research, summarization, and organization. The project is still in progress, and we are interviewing lawyers to understand their needs before building further.
